Privacy Policy
ParauPistes is a small extension that shows hints for the Paraulògic game.

It doesn't collect any data. Nothing you do is tracked, stored, or sent anywhere. The extension reads the game page in your browser only to work out the hints, and that stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party services.

If you have a question, open an issue at https://github.com/Seifer23/ParauPistes/issues
